# Roadmap

Ideas for future versions, in no particular order. Requests are welcome as [issues](https://github.com/Tufein/DogmatixPlus/issues).

- **More providers in the content catalogue**, based on requests, each with clear attribution and console mapping.
- **More save sync for standalone emulators**, such as Dolphin's GCI folders and Wii saves, and other emulators whose saves live in card or NAND images.
- **Covers for specific launchers**: the layouts come from public documentation, so tell us if your launcher reads covers from a place none of the layouts or your own pattern can describe, such as a different image type or a launcher-specific database.
