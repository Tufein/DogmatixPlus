# Roadmap

Ideas for future versions, in no particular order. Requests are welcome as [issues](https://github.com/Tufein/DogmatixPlus/issues).

- **Full multi-file transfer to RomM or a NAS**, keeping the folder structure and checking the complete package.
- **Package export with a preview** of the files, the space needed and the destination; shared files stay safe and saves are never bundled by accident.
- **More sources in the content catalogue**, based on requests, with clear provider attribution and console mapping.
- **Handing a game over to another handheld**, for emulator and save combinations that work, with the way back shown first.
- **Covers for iiSU and Daijishō** once their cover folders are known.
- **Save sync for DuckStation and PPSSPP** (memory cards and save-data folders).
