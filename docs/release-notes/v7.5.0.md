**Dogmatix+ 7.5.0** (pre-release) — more control over downloads: from the second screen, from the notifications, and around the battery and heat. Builds on 7.0.0.

### Second screen
- **Pause all / Resume all** and a **pause or resume button per download** on the second (bottom) screen of dual-screen handhelds, with clear "Paused" states.

### Downloads that look after your handheld
- **Pause on low battery** (off the charger, below a limit you choose, 20 % by default) and **pause when the device is hot** (from 45 °C or when Android reports overheating). They go on by themselves; the Downloads screen and the notification say what they wait for. *Settings → Download schedule*.
- **Pause all now really pauses**: running downloads that can continue later stop and wait (a web download keeps its partial file, a torrent its data). Downloads that cannot be resumed safely (unpacking, debrid, servers without resume) finish first, so no progress is thrown away.

### Notifications
- The downloads notification has **Pause all / Resume all / Stop all**; tapping it opens Downloads.
- A finished game gets its own quiet notification with **Open in the library** and **Open the downloads**.

### Best source
- When several sources have the same game, the app picks the **most reliable and fastest one** it has seen, and a download that keeps failing **switches once to the next source** ("Switched to …"). Source cards show their track record. *Settings → Downloads → Pick the best source* (on by default).

### Good to know
- Updating from 7.0.0 keeps everything; the database does not change.
- Checked by the unit tests and a debug and release build in CI. **Not tried on a real device yet**, so the second-screen buttons and the battery and heat rules have not been seen on a handheld.
