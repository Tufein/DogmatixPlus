**Dogmatix+ 2.6.0** — no more force quits with big download queues, Dogmatix+ tiles in Cocoon, and a new icon.

### Fixed
- **Force quits with big queues.** Starting a few hundred downloads at once (for example with *Download everything shown*) could make Android report the app as not responding and close it. The queue has been rebuilt: starting many downloads is now one quick step, and waiting downloads no longer keep the phone busy. Tested with 500 queued downloads while switching away from the app and back: no force quit, and the list stays smooth.
- **The download notification** no longer causes the app to be closed when downloads start and finish close together.
- A download started twice at the same moment (a tap and a bulk start) now starts once.

### New
- **Cocoon**: add Dogmatix+ to Cocoon as tiles — one per console, per saved view, or for Downloads. In Cocoon, open *Add Games* and choose the Android shortcuts; *Settings → Cocoon* in Dogmatix+ shows the steps. The same shortcuts appear when you long-press the app icon.
- **New icon**: a game cartridge with dog ears and a download arrow, in the app's orange. It also follows your wallpaper colours on Android 13+ when themed icons are on. Run *Configure ES-DE* again to get the new banner there too.
- **Share diagnostics** now also says why the app last stopped, which helps with bug reports.

### Good to know
- Updating from 2.5.0 keeps everything; there is no database change.
- Tip for Cocoon: use these tiles rather than the `.dgmtx` shortcut files — Cocoon would list those files as games.
- Tested on an emulator. Cocoon itself was not installed for the test; the shortcuts and the picker it uses are standard Android and were checked on their own.
