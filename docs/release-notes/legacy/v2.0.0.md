**Dogmatix+ 2.0.0** — rescans that skip what did not change, new games, collections, Switch updates and DLC, DAT checks, QR sharing, a widget and updates from inside the app.

### Scanning
- **Rescans are much faster.** A source whose list did not change is skipped: no parsing, no rewriting of the library. Six unchanged sources of 4,000 games went from 18 seconds to 2. *Library overview → Full rescan* still reads everything.
- **A source that fails keeps its games** until it works again, instead of vanishing from the library.
- **Reserve addresses**: give a web source one or more other addresses; when the first one fails, the next is tried.
- **Scan in the background** (*Settings → Scan sources automatically*): daily (or every 12 h, two days, a week), by default only on Wi-Fi, while charging and at night. You get a notification when new games turn up.

### Library
- **New games** get a *New* badge for two weeks; the *New* filter and *Newest first* sort show them.
- **Collections**: make your own lists next to the favourites (details card → *Collections*).
- **Download everything shown**: one button next to the result count; it shows how many games, how big, and whether it fits first. Optionally only the best version of each game.
- **Nintendo Switch**: files with a title ID are recognised as game, update or DLC. The details card shows whether a newer update or missing DLC is in your sources; *Library tools → Switch updates & DLC* lists them for all your games.
- **DAT check**: import a No-Intro or Redump DAT per console and check your files — good, good but wrongly named (rename with one tap), unknown, and which games you are missing.

### Downloads
- **The speed limit really works now.** Before, it never slowed anything down (a unit mix-up). It now applies to all downloads together, torrents included, and can be lifted at night.

### Also new
- **File explorer**: rename, move and unpack.
- **Share your sources as a QR code** (*Sources → Share*); the other device reads it with its camera or from a picture.
- **Updates from inside the app**: *Check for updates* can now download, check and install the new version. This works from 2.0.0 on; to get 2.0.0 itself, install it from this page once.
- **Home-screen widget** with your downloads and the newest games.
- **Bold focus ring** for TV and handheld use.

### Good to know
- Updating from 1.3.0 keeps everything. The **first rescan after updating reads every source once**; after that unchanged sources are skipped.
- Some suggestions turned out to exist already — Wi-Fi-only downloads, favourites and gamepad navigation. These were extended (collections next to favourites, the speed limit fixed and shared, a bold focus ring), not rebuilt.
- Tested on an emulator against a local test server, not against your own RomM server.
