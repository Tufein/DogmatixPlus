**Dogmatix+ 5.0.0** — a new look for the whole app, and the cloud where you play: RomM on every game, saves per game, an encrypted backup to your own WebDAV server, and RetroAchievements progress. Layout, navigation and button layout are unchanged.

### A new look
- **Depth and colour**: panels with a soft top light, a faint glow of your accent colour behind every screen, and console colours (Nintendo red, PlayStation blue, Game Boy green…) on chips, covers and charts. Light, dark, true black, all accents and Material You.
- **Focus you can see from the couch**: a tonal fill, a ring and a soft halo; touch gets press feedback too.
- **Covers** in the details card, the Downloads list, the wishlist, the second screen and, if you like, in the library list: the RomM cover, libretro box art, a stored metadata image, or a tile with the console's colour.
- **Charts and icons**: rings, a segmented storage bar and column charts in Tools and in the Downloads header; around 80 new icons instead of text signs.
- **Calmer motion**: screens fade and slide, rows expand, the queue moves into place. *Settings → Look and controls* has **Animations**, **Background glow** and **Covers in the library list**, with a preview.
- **A new Downloads header** (speed, time left, space needed against free space), **source cards with their health**, empty states with a button, and a themed **second screen, widget and shortcut picker**.

### Cloud hub
- **Settings → Cloud** shows RomM, save sync, cloud backup, device sync, RetroAchievements and Debrid on one screen, each with its main action. A small **cloud icon in the top bar** shows idle, syncing or "needs you".

### RomM on every game
- **On RomM** in the details card: summary, genres, year, rating and screenshots from your server, plus your **play status** and **rating**, which are written back to RomM.
- **Favourites in step with RomM**, both ways (optional).
- **BIOS from RomM**: *Tools → BIOS check* fetches the missing files from the server's firmware. Every file is checked (MD5) and a file that is already there is never replaced.

### Cloud saves
- **Per game**, in the details card: the saves and states on the server (with the state's screenshot) and the safety copies on the device, each with **Restore**; **Upload now** sends a newer file from this device.
- **Continue playing** on Home: the games last saved on any device, or last played in ES-DE.

### Your own cloud (WebDAV)
- **Encrypted backup** to Nextcloud, ownCloud, Synology, Koofr or any WebDAV server: AES-256-GCM with a key made from your passphrase, daily on Wi-Fi while charging, a list of backups with restore, and a number to keep. The server password and the passphrase stay on this device; they are not in backups or diagnostics. Lose the passphrase and the backups cannot be opened.
- **Device sync**: favourites, wishlist and collections are merged between your handhelds. A sync that would remove many items waits for your OK.
- A server with a **self-signed certificate** can be trusted after you have seen its fingerprint.

### RetroAchievements
- **Progress per game**: a ring and badges (earned, hardcore, missable) in the details card; your **profile** (points, rank, recently played) in the hub and in *Tools → RetroAchievements*.

### Good to know
- Updating from 4.0.0 keeps everything. The database gets one small table for found covers (version 13).
- Checked by the unit tests and a debug build in CI. **Not tried on a real device, nor against a real RomM, WebDAV or RetroAchievements server yet.** Try the cloud backup with a test folder first. If something does not connect, the hub and the cloud screens say why; a diagnostics report (*Settings → Diagnostics*) helps with the rest.
