# Dogmatix+ 1.6.0

Regular release; originally published as **v7.0.0**. The preserved APK reports `7.0.0` (Android build 28).

**Dogmatix+ 7.0.0** — keep collections on your device, free up space, descriptions for your launcher, and a lot of polish. Layout and buttons are unchanged.

### New
- **Keep a collection on your device** (*Tools → Collections*): new games in it download by themselves, a few per run, only when there is room. Nothing is deleted automatically.
- **Free up space** (*Tools*): games you never played, biggest first. Favourites, collection games, RomM saves and achievements are protected. Remove them, or remove and put them on the wishlist, after a confirmation that lists every file.
- **Descriptions for your launcher** (*Tools*): description, genre, year and rating into ES-DE's gamelist.xml and Pegasus' metadata.txt, without touching what is there. A backup copy is made first.
- **Best games per console** (*Tools*, needs a RetroAchievements key): the most-loved games, what you have, and a button for the ones you can still get.
- **Better version available** (*Tools*): a newer revision, a final instead of a beta, or a good dump, for games you have. The old file goes only after the new one finished and checked out.
- **A weekly digest** (optional, *Settings*), a **Quick Settings tile** to pause or resume downloads, launcher shortcuts (Surprise me, Downloads, Search), and **your year in games** with a card to share.

### Polish
- **Settings**: every row has an icon in the same style, in the same place, also in the cloud screens.
- **Second screen** shows your own icon instead of the mascot.

### Good to know
- Updating from 6.0.0 keeps everything. The database does not change.
- Checked by the unit tests and a debug and release build in CI. **Not tried on a real device, nor against a real RomM, WebDAV or RetroAchievements server yet.** Try *Free up space* and *Descriptions for your launcher* on a small console first.
